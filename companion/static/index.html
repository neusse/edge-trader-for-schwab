Paper equity entry

Choose a broker-held bracket with one target and fixed stop, or an unprotected simple entry with no exits. Existing order-free positions can receive OCO exits, a standalone trailing stop, a market close, a partial market close, or add shares from the Positions list. Working OCO stop and target prices can be adjusted from Working orders. A trailing stop has no paired target; partial closes and added shares have no new stop until a new OCO is accepted. Options and two targets are unavailable in this form.

Paper entries are disarmed.

Paper option discovery

Browse one expiration and type at a time. Alpaca option quotes and Greeks are indicative and may be delayed or absent. Level 2 can buy a long call or put; level 1 can sell a covered call or cash-secured put. Each is a reviewed limit order without an attached stop or target. Short options can be assigned.

Choose an underlying and expiration.

Select a tradable contract. Paper orders must be armed above.

Paper vertical entry

Level 3 only. Choose a long and short contract from the same chain. The signed net limit is positive for a debit and negative for a credit. Both legs are sent as one broker multi-leg order. No spread stop or target is attached after fill.

Load one option chain, then select two strikes.
